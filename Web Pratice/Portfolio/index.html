About the doctor

Fourteen years of listening before prescribing.

Dr. Aditi Verma founded Sanjeevani Health Clinic on one belief: good medicine starts with a doctor who has time to actually listen. She trained in internal medicine, but her real specialty is continuity — knowing your history well enough to catch what's changed.

Her patients range from newborns' first checkups to grandparents managing decades-long conditions. Between them, she has built a practice where nobody is "just a number in the waiting room."
